# Public catalog (/)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Recoletos members on their phone, often at the shelf or before fetching a book. Job: is it here, where, who has it; take or return in one tap plus a name.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every book is its library loan card (ficha de préstamo); the page is the card drawer. Refuses the product-card grid and the data table.

OWN-WORLD: White page; fichas on a pale brand-tint panel (#EEF5F4, dark: #162124), radius 12, no shadow. Ink near-black teal. Brand teal #027A88 only on the location line and the one filled action; mint #2BB39A marks a free copy. Nunito (rounded, echoes the wordmark) for everything; Caveat only for borrower names, written on the card's ruled lines. Recoletos wordmark top-left.

STORY: The visitor types a title, sees the ficha, reads the shelf in teal, sees one ruled line per copy: a handwritten name and date, or a blank "libre" line. Tapping "Me lo llevo" turns the first blank line into a field to write their name; tapping a name offers "Lo devuelvo".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: 56px bar (wordmark, "Encargadas" link); h1 "Biblioteca de Recoletos" 28px with the live fact line (libros, sitios, prestados ahora); 52px search; one row with Ubicación and Categoría selects; first ficha starts above 60% of the screen. Desktop: centred 1100px column, fichas in 2-3 columns.

FORM: ficha de préstamo, position 3 of 7 on the ranked list (dealt lead); seed key 21dae39d. Taste app style editorial-greeting, seed 2029278391.

FINISH: unreviewed and unverified until the finish reviewer returns ship.
